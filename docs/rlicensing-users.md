Licensing Users for Customer Assist

Users that will become agents or supervisors of Customer Assist queues will need to be licensed.  You can do this one at a time, using a Webex group or groups synchronized from your company directory. In this lab you will create a Webex Group

<p class="lab-step" markdown="1">170. Under the management section of Control Hub, click Groups, then Webex groups then Create a group.</p>

![Screenshot](./assets/image24.png)

<p class="lab-step" markdown="1">171. Enter the Group Name and then an (optional) description, press Next</p>

![Screenshot](./assets/image25.png)

<p class="lab-step" markdown="1">172. Select Add manually and then search and add the following users and then click Save</p>
